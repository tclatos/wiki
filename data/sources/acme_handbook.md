# Acme Robotics — Engineering Handbook

## Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue **25 days** of paid vacation per year.
Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days; anything beyond that
is paid out in January.

## On-Call Rotation

The on-call rotation is weekly and swaps every Monday at 10:00 UTC.
The primary responder carries the pager; the secondary covers escalations.

| Role      | Team          | Compensation    |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Primary   | rotates weekly| 2 compensatory days per quarter |
| Secondary | rotates weekly| 1 compensatory day per quarter |

## Expense Reimbursement

Expenses are submitted through the finance portal within **30 days** of
purchase. Claims over 500 EUR require manager pre-approval; claims over
2,000 EUR additionally require the finance director's signature.

## Security Incident Response

Severity 1 incidents (data breach, full outage) must be reported to the
security duty officer within **15 minutes** of detection. The postmortem
is due within 5 business days and follows the blameless review template.
